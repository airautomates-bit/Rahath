How it works

What happens after you call us

Here is what to expect once you book with us.

01

Book and get ready

Pick a package and we confirm your place. We help with your documents and hold a briefing so you know what to do before you leave.

02

Travel with the group

You fly out together. We look after the hotels, buses and meals, so you don't have to.

03

Perform Umrah and Ziyarat

Your guide takes you through each ritual. You also visit important places in Makkah and Madinah and learn their history.
