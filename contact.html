Your privacy How we use your details

What we ask for

Your name, phone number, package, how many people are travelling and a good time to call, plus any note you add.

Where it goes

Your request comes straight to our team and is kept in a private record that only our staff can see. We use it to call you back and plan your trip.

Passports and payments

This website never asks for your passport or money. We collect visa documents in person, and you pay in cash or by bank transfer.

Ads and your choices

We may use your phone number to help our Google ads reach people like you. We never sell your details. To change, delete or opt out, call or WhatsApp us on +94 72 532 9242.

Like most websites, we use cookies to count visits. They don't tell us who you are.
